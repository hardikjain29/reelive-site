## In the app

1. Open Reelive and tap your picture at the top of Home to open Settings.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

## Without the app

Email <a href={`mailto:${OPERATOR.email}?subject=Delete%20my%20account`}>{OPERATOR.email}</a> with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address of your Reelive account if you can. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account.

## What gets deleted

- your reels and the photos and videos you added;
- your account details (the Apple or Google account ID, email address and name);
- your Pro status, ratings, feedback and usage data, including the copies held by our analytics and subscription providers.

## What stays

- a scrambled (hashed) record that your phone has had its free reels, without your account, so free reels can't be claimed again by signing up again;
- records of purchases, without your account, for as long as tax law requires;
- server logs and crash reports, until they expire after at most 90 days.

**Subscriptions aren't cancelled automatically.** If you have Pro Yearly, cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Apple will keep charging you.
