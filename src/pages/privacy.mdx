## 3. What we process and why

### Your account

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account ID from them, and your email address and name if you share them. With Apple you can hide your real email address. From Google we also receive a link to your profile picture. We use this to create your account, keep it secure and keep your reels with you.

Sign in with Google asks only for basic sign-in information (Google's openid, email and profile scopes). We don't access any other Google data, such as your contacts, Drive or Gmail, and we don't share what we receive from Google with anyone, except the service providers that help us run Reelive (section 4).

If you look around without signing in, the app creates a guest account with a random ID. Guest accounts that never make a reel are deleted after 30 days.

**Legal basis:** our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). **Kept:** until you delete your account.

### Your photos, videos and reels

The photos and videos you add are uploaded to our storage. To make your reel:

- an automatic content check looks at each photo and at frames of each video for sexual content first. A file it flags isn't used in your reel, and we may look at it to decide whether it breaks our Terms;
- our AI analysis looks at them: what's in each photo and clip, which ones are near-duplicates, and which moments are best;
- our rendering service puts the chosen moments into the template and produces the finished video.

We store your uploads, the results of the analysis, the finished reel, the template you used and any text you type into it. We don't use your photos or videos to train AI models. OpenAI, which runs the analysis, doesn't train its models on data sent through its API, and keeps it for at most 30 days to detect abuse.

**Legal basis:** our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for the content check, our legitimate interest in keeping illegal and harmful content off Reelive (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). **Kept:** until you delete the reel or your account. Uploads that never finish are deleted automatically after about a day. A record that a file was flagged (when, and the check's score, not the file) is kept until you delete your account, so we can see repeated uploads of such content.

### Free reels and protection against abuse

Each phone gets its free reels only once. To make that work, the app sends a random identifier stored on your phone. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) form of it and of your IP address, together with how many free reels were given. This record isn't deleted with your account, so free reels can't be claimed again by deleting an account and signing up again.

We also count requests per IP address for a short time, to stop one network from creating endless accounts or overloading the service. These counters expire after the limit's time window, at most a day.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the free offer and keeping the service available (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). **Kept:** the free-reel record for as long as we offer free reels; request counters for at most a day.

### Purchases

Reelive Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription: RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase information and your Reelive account ID. We store whether you have Pro and until when, and a record of each purchase event (product, date and store transaction IDs).

**Legal basis:** our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legal obligations to keep business records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). **Kept:** your Pro status until you delete your account. Purchase records are kept without a link to your account for as long as tax and commercial law require, up to 10 years.

### Usage data

We record how the app is used: which screens you open and what you do there, for example which templates you look at, the reels you make, and what you type into the template search. Each event carries your account ID, the app version, your phone's platform and your language and region setting. It doesn't include your email address, advertising IDs, device IDs or your location. The app keeps these events on your phone for a short time until it can send them to us.

We use this to understand what works, what doesn't and what to fix. We also send a copy to PostHog, our analytics provider, which hosts the data in the EU.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in improving Reelive (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). **Kept:** up to 13 months in our systems. PostHog keeps its copy for as long as its plan keeps events, currently 1 year. When you delete your account, both copies are deleted.

**You can object at any time.** Email <a href={`mailto:${OPERATOR.email}`}>{OPERATOR.email}</a> and we'll stop recording your usage data.

### Crash reports

When the app or our servers hit an error, our error-tracking service, Sentry, receives a report: what went wrong, the app version, the device model and system version, and your account ID.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping Reelive working (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). **Kept:** up to 90 days.

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your phone's push token so we can tell you when your reel is ready. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple Push Notification service.

**Legal basis:** your permission (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in your iPhone's Settings. **Kept:** until you turn notifications off or delete your account.

### Feedback and ratings

If you rate a reel or send feedback in the app, we store your rating, the reasons you pick, your message, and the app version, system version, device model and language. We get a short alert when new feedback arrives, which doesn't include your message or who you are. We may answer you by email.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in improving Reelive and answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). **Kept:** until you delete your account.

### Emails to support

When you write to {OPERATOR.email}, we use your email address and what you send us to answer. Our email address forwards to our mailbox at Google (Gmail).

**Legal basis:** our contract with you, or our legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). **Kept:** as long as needed to help you and handle any follow-up.

### App updates

The app checks for and downloads updates from Expo's update service. Like any download, this request includes your IP address and basic information about the app and your phone.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and up to date (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Server logs

Our servers log requests, including IP addresses and times, for security and troubleshooting.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in running a secure service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). **Kept:** up to 30 days.

### This website

reelive.app is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub processes your IP address and details of the request (the page, the time, your browser) to deliver the page and protect the service. The site's fonts are loaded from the site itself.

To see how the site is used, we use PostHog: which pages are visited, the site you came from, how far you scroll, and which buttons and questions you click. **No cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device.** Each page visit gets a random ID that exists only while the page is open, so separate visits aren't linked. PostHog uses your IP address to estimate your approximate location (country, region and city) and then discards it: we've set PostHog not to store IP addresses. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, nothing is recorded.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in providing a secure website and understanding how it's used, so we can improve it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). **Kept:** website usage data for as long as PostHog's plan keeps events, currently 1 year. You can object at any time by emailing us.

## 5. How long we keep it

Most of your data is kept only as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in Settings, we delete your uploads, reels, account details, ratings, feedback and usage data, and ask PostHog and RevenueCat to delete theirs. What stays:

- the hashed free-reel record, without your account (see section 3);
- purchase records, without your account, for as long as the law requires;
- server logs and crash reports, until they expire.

- **receive** it in a portable format (Art. 20);
- **withdraw** a permission you gave, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3));
- **complain** to a data protection authority (Art. 77), for example where you live, or the one responsible for us: {OPERATOR.authority}.

## 7. Automated decisions

Reelive's AI decides which of your photos and clips go into a reel and where, and the content check can leave a file out of a reel. These choices don't have legal or similarly significant effects on you, so they aren't automated decisions in the sense of Art. 22 GDPR. If you don't like a result, you can try another template. Blocking an account is never automatic: a person decides.

## 8. Children

Reelive isn't meant for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.
